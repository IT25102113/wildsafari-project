National Reserves

Discover Sri Lanka's Iconic Havens

Home to the world's highest density of leopards and majestic Asian elephant herds.

Yala National Park
Block 1 & 2 • Southern Province

World-famous for Panthera pardus kotiya, sloth bears, coastal lagoons, and vibrant bird sanctuaries.

Wilpattu Park
Ancient Villus • North Western

The 'Land of Lakes' known for tranquil untouched forest trails, deer, barking deer, and elusive predators.

Udawalawe Sanctuary
Elephant Reserve • Sabaragamuwa

Guaranteed elephant sightings in expansive grasslands surrounding the reservoir and the Elephant Transit Home.

Minneriya National Park
Dry Zone • North Central

Home to 'The Gathering' where up to 300 wild elephants congregate on lush tank beds during the dry season.
