About WildSafari

Pioneering Sustainable Wildlife Safaris in Sri Lanka

WildSafari is Sri Lanka’s premier digital safari expedition ecosystem. We bridge the Department of Wildlife Conservation (DWC) guidelines with certified wildlife naturalists, specialized 4x4 safari fleets, and ethical tourists worldwide.

🌿
Ethical Conservation

Committed to protecting Ceylon's endangered leopards, Asian elephants, and sloth bears through responsible non-intrusive safari tracks.

🧭
Certified Naturalists

Every expedition is escorted by accredited field guides with deep tracking knowledge across Yala, Wilpattu, Udawalawe, and Minneriya.

🚙
High-Elevation Cruisers

Custom-engineered safari 4x4 vehicles designed for ultimate safety, photography vantage points, and comfort across all national terrain.
